# Written Ethics Self-Assessment

## Study assessed

Audience-calibrated medical LLM safety assessment: a paired multi-model benchmark study.

## Determination

This study used publicly released, deidentified benchmark questions and machine-generated text outputs, with no human participants, patient contact, intervention, or processing of personal data. Under the Swedish Ethical Review Act (2003:460), such research does not require ethical review, and no application to the Swedish Ethical Review Authority was made.

## Basis for the determination

- The source questions were obtained from a publicly released, deidentified benchmark dataset.
- The study generated and evaluated machine-produced text; it did not recruit, intervene with, observe, or collect data from patients, clinicians, reviewers, or other human participants.
- The LLM judge-panel ratings were machine-generated. Previously supplied evaluator workbooks were analysed as AI-assisted or deterministic evaluator outputs, not as prospectively collected human-participant research data or independent physician reference ratings.
- The study did not process identifiable or coded personal data, biological material, or information collected through physical or psychological intervention.
- The work was an offline methodological evaluation and did not influence patient care.

## Governance

The determination was made by the corresponding author and is retained as part of the dated replication package. The study reports evaluator-assigned outcomes and does not claim that the LLM panel, holistic evaluator, or deterministic engine constitutes clinical ground truth.
