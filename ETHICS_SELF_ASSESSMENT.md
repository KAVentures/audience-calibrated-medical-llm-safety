# Study-specific research ethics and data governance self-assessment

**Study:** *Evaluator-dependent conclusions in audience-calibrated medical LLM safety assessment: a paired multi-model benchmark study*  
**Author:** Koyar Afrasyab, MD, Kinvectum AB, Sweden  
**Assessment date:** 18 July 2026 (updated for study-specific clarity)

## Study population and materials

This was a computational evaluation of 100 sampled publicly released Real-POCQi
clinical question topics and machine-generated answers. No patients were
recruited, contacted, treated, or exposed to any intervention; identifiable
patient records were not collected by the study. The underlying questions
were sourced from an existing public release and remain subject to that
dataset's governance and licence. The study did not attempt re-identification.

The primary panel consisted of four LLM judges, with blinded prompt/response
inputs and no newly recruited physician rating panel. The author, a physician,
reports manually rating ten examples used for an exploratory
physician-calibrated AI-assisted evaluator. These are **not independent
clinician-validation data** and cannot supply a human clinical reference
standard. The author is also a human contributor to the research process,
so descriptions such as "no human participants" refer to the absence of
recruited study subjects, not an absence of any human expert input.

## Swedish ethical-review assessment

The author previously assessed this retrospective computational benchmark as
outside mandatory review under the Swedish Ethical Review Act (2003:460),
given the absence of clinical interventions, human biological material, or
planned processing of identifiable research subjects' sensitive health data.
No ethics approval was requested or obtained.

This is **an investigator self-assessment only**, not a determination or an
exemption certificate from Etikprövningsmyndigheten, and not a legal opinion.
Institutional or publisher requirements should be verified independently
when applicable. Ordinary author information and administrative records, if
retained, must be handled under applicable privacy rules.

## Integrity and data access

The released replication package includes deidentified input prompts,
model outputs, evaluator results, blinding keys, generation metadata, and
analysis programs. Any clinical-source dataset restrictions continue to
apply. Model-provider credentials and personally identifying administrative
information must not be released.

No result in this study establishes an individual patient's clinical outcome,
a population rate of harmful medical advice, or that one LLM is clinically
safer than another.

**Status:** self-assessment; no formal institutional or authority decision
is documented here.
